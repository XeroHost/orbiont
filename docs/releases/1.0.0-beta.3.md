# Orbiont 1.0.0-beta.3

Make Orbiont your own with new appearance controls and a more reliable interface.

## Personalize your launcher

- Choose from 15 accent colors. The logo, buttons, selections and startup
  background follow your chosen color, independently of the light, dark or OLED theme.
- Switch between Comfortable and Compact interface density. Compact is marked
  as Beta and adjusts spacing in the library and recent instances and worlds.
- Choose Small, Normal or Large interface sizing to scale text and controls together.
- Control reduced motion and icon animations independently. Animated icons can
  stay enabled while other transitions and automatic card rotation are reduced.
- Preview appearance changes before saving, or cancel to restore your previous settings.

## Reliability and interaction fixes

- Fixed the blank window that could appear when changing interface size.
- Improved layout measurement and scrolling to prevent ResizeObserver feedback loops.
- Fixed skin and player-head cache cleanup, and removed missing texture references
  from the bundled player models.
- Improved keyboard focus and selection controls in appearance settings.
- Restored the original right-panel background and kept its content visible.
- Added an independent option to bring Orbiont to the foreground when Minecraft
  closes, including official Bedrock sessions launched through Orbiont on Windows.
- Added the new interface text across all 33 supported languages.

## Download

Download the installers for Windows, macOS and Linux from
[xerohost.net/orbiont](https://www.xerohost.net/orbiont#downloads).

Bedrock integration requires Windows and an officially licensed installation.
Orbiont remains in beta; feedback and bug reports are welcome.
